MATHEMATICS

A fraction is a part of a whole

Cut one orange into four equal pieces. One piece is one quarter, written 1/4. Two pieces are 2/4, which is the same as one half.

Try it: What fraction is three of the four pieces?

BASIC SCIENCE

Plants need more than water

Plants use light, water and carbon dioxide to make food. Their roots take in water and minerals from the soil.

Try it: Why might a plant kept in a dark room grow poorly?

ENGLISH LANGUAGE

A verb tells you what happens

A verb can describe an action, such as read, walk or sing. In “Amina reads a book”, reads is the verb.

Try it: Find the verb in “Chidi kicks the ball”.
