# Test questions — Phase 1

40 questions. Each speaker translates them **naturally** into their language (how they'd actually say it, not word-for-word), writes down exactly what they said, and records it.

## Recording instructions (send this to the speakers)
- **Format:** WAV, mono. 16 kHz is ideal, but any sample rate is fine. Phone recordings in .m4a are OK too; we'll convert them.
- **Length:** one question per file, 2–10 seconds. Leave half a second of silence at the start and end.
- **Where:** a quiet room, phone about 20–30 cm from the mouth. A few can be recorded with normal home noise (TV, generator) so we test real conditions. Mark those as noisy.
- **File names:** `<language>_<speaker>_q<number>.wav`, e.g. `yoruba_tunde_q07.wav`, `hausa_amina_q23.wav`, `english_keside_q01.wav`
- **Also send:** a text file or spreadsheet with the exact words said for each file, **with tone marks** for Yoruba and Igbo. We need it to check whether the ASR heard them correctly.

## Everyday & greetings
1. Good morning, how are you today?
2. What is your name, and what can you do?
3. Can you greet my mother? She just came back from the market.
4. How do I greet an elder respectfully?
5. Tell me something to make me smile today.

## Food & home
6. How do I cook jollof rice?
7. What can I cook with yam, eggs and tomatoes?
8. How long should I boil beans before they are soft?
9. What foods are good for someone who has malaria?
10. How do I keep tomatoes from spoiling quickly?

## Health
11. What are the signs of malaria?
12. How much water should I drink in a day?
13. My child has a fever. What should I do before we get to the hospital?
14. Is it good to exercise every morning?
15. What should I do if someone is bleeding from a cut?

## Money & work
16. How can I start saving money every month?
17. Give me an idea for a small business I can start with fifty thousand naira.
18. What is the difference between a savings account and a current account?
19. How do I write a good letter to apply for a job?
20. How can a farmer protect his crops from pests?

## Knowledge & culture
21. Who was the first president of Nigeria?
22. How many states are there in Nigeria?
23. Tell me a proverb and explain what it means.
24. What is the capital of Nigeria?
25. Tell me a short story for children.
26. Why do we celebrate Independence Day on the first of October?
27. What is the meaning of my name, Chidinma?
28. Tell me about the Argungu fishing festival.

## Maths & quick facts
29. What is twenty-five plus seventeen?
30. If I buy three loaves of bread at eight hundred naira each, how much will I pay?
31. How many days are in a year?
32. What is half of one thousand five hundred?

## Advice & conversation
33. How can I help my child do better in school?
34. My friend is angry with me. How do I settle the matter?
35. What should I do when I cannot sleep at night?
36. How do I prepare for a wedding on a small budget?
37. Can you help me plan my day tomorrow?

## Edge cases
38. What will the weather be like tomorrow in Lagos? *(it has no internet, so it should say it doesn't know rather than invent an answer)*
39. Please speak slowly and repeat what you just said.
40. Thank you, goodbye.

## Bonus: mixed-language (optional, 5–10 per speaker)
Say a few questions the way people naturally mix languages, e.g. English words inside a Yoruba/Hausa/Igbo sentence. Name them `..._mix01.wav` and so on.
